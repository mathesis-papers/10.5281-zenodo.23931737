# Contribute to the mathematics and formalization

The shared requests below are the coordination points for the exact scopes in the technical chapter. The family result already has a private checked proof: its task is a stable public interface and the unordered-subset bridge. The original positive real orthonormal theorem needs no duplicate proof request.

- [Signed upper comparison](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/117)
- [Public coordinate-family comparison](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/118)
- [Hermitian complex comparison](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/119)
- [Signed two-sided comparison](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/120)
- [Arbitrary-degree repeated lifts](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/121)
- [Constant-weight equality and sharpness](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/122)
- [Distance exponent](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/123)

Read the relevant statement and proof before proposing work, and coordinate the exact clause in its canonical issue. Develop a focused branch in your own contributor fork and submit directly to [upstream trureturing `dev`](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/blob/dev/docs/CONTRIBUTING.md) under the existing admission, review and CI rules. Preserve the paper authors’ and prior authors’ mathematical attribution. Link the same request from the PR; a Mathesis-fork merge is not a prerequisite.

After an upstream result is merged and frozen, compare its complete statement before changing an evidence citation. Keep broader clauses open when only a special case has been formalized. A GitHub issue, successful CI or merge is distinct from an independent Mathesis verifier receipt and creates no platform credit. The [public Mathesis contribution route](https://mathesis-omega-research.apps.chrono-ai.fun/contribute) explains the separate receipt process.

For content maintenance, keep Chinese and English chapter order, result attributes, proofs and media references aligned. Run the installed paper consistency check and `./tbook check`. Use the existing Beamer and book-film interfaces for rendering. A narrated film’s real measured timeline must replace its estimate before final rendering; inspect both editions and the actual playable film before publication.
